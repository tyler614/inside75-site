Inside 75 Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Inside 75 is a golf game in testing with a small group of friends.

In short: to make swing reading more accurate, the app can send your swings and a log of how the game went to the developer. This is on unless you turn it off, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings ("Share swings"). There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

What the app sends when "Share swings" is on

  • Your swings: for each swing, the motion readings from your clip sensor around it (a few seconds before and after), how the app read it (speed, face, path and so on), what happened to the ball, and the game settings at the time. For swings made by touch, the path of your finger on the screen.
  • A log of the session: what happened in the game (screens opened, shots, messages shown, errors) and how smoothly it ran.
  • About your device: its model, operating system, screen size and language, and the app's version.
  • Who it came from: a random number the app makes when it is installed (not linked to you or your device's own identifiers), and, only if you type them in Settings, a nickname, your handicap and what you play off (a mat, carpet or grass).

The app does not send your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, or any advertising identifier. Wi-Fi network names and passwords are removed before anything is sent, and a file that still contains one is never sent.

Problem reports

If you tap "Report a problem", the app sends what you write, a picture of the screen (with anything you mark on it), and the recent session logs. The clip's motion readings are included only if you tick that box. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Tuning updates

When it starts, the app may download a small file of updated numbers for reading swings. Only that download request is sent: it carries no information about you.

Where it goes and who sees it

Shared data is stored with Supabase (a database service, in the United States). Only the developer can read it, and it is used only to improve how Inside 75 reads swings and plays. Like any internet service, the storage provider sees your device's internet address when the app connects.

What stays on your device

Your bag of clubs and each clip's setup, your settings, recent clip recordings (capped in size, oldest deleted first) and Wi-Fi passwords you typed during clip setup (stored encrypted). These are not sent anywhere except as described above.

Turning it off and deleting your data

  • Turn off "Share swings" in Settings: nothing more is sent, and anything still waiting to be sent is deleted from the device.
  • To have what was already sent deleted, email helveytr@gmail.com with your nickname (or roughly when you played) and it will be deleted.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it kept on your device.

Bluetooth and your local network

Bluetooth is used only to set up clip sensors. During play the app talks to your clips over your own Wi-Fi, which is why an iPad asks for Local Network access; that traffic stays on your network.

Children

Inside 75 is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If what the app sends changes, this page and its date will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Inside 75: helveytr@gmail.com.
